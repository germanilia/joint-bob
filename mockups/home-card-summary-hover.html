Home cards · summary on hover

Five ways to show a Needs you card's latest summary when the mouse is on it. Hover the cards in each option. All use the same summary (Goal, Done so far, Current state, Open questions) plus when it was last updated. Touch screens show none of them, because tapping a card already opens its detail sheet.

A · Cover the card built now

The summary covers the whole card while hovered, and grows downward if it is longer. It is read-only: clicks pass through to the hidden buttons.

+ No layout shift, nothing outside the card− Buttons are hidden while you read; it can't scroll

B · Popover beside the card recommended

After a short delay (0.25 s), a floating panel opens next to the card: to the right for the left column and to the left for the right column. You can move the mouse into it to scroll a long summary. The card itself does not change.

+ Buttons stay visible; long summaries scroll; it's what most apps do− Covers the neighbouring card while it's open

C · Expand in place

The card grows: the short question gives way to the full summary between the title and the buttons. The summary is capped at 240 px and scrolls when it is longer, so the card never gets taller than that. Cards in the same row stay where they are.

+ Everything stays reachable; nothing floats; long summaries scroll− The grid moves under the mouse as the card grows

D · Peek inside the card

The card keeps its size. The title and question area turns into a compact, scrollable summary with labelled rows, and the buttons stay below it.

+ No movement, buttons visible, wheel scrolls it− A small window: only about three rows show at once

E · Shared summary pane

One panel under Needs you shows the summary of whichever card the mouse is on, and keeps the last one until you hover another card.

+ Calm; cards never change; room for long summaries− Takes space on Home all the time; your eye has to move away from the card
