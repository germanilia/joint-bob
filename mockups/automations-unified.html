Mockup · Automations

Scheduled tasks and webhooks become one thing: an automation

An automation is instructions, an agent and its context, started by a trigger: a schedule or a webhook call. The same editor makes and changes every automation, inside the Automations tab. Scheduled automations get what only webhooks had (files, skills, memory, secret accounts), and webhooks get what only schedules had (machine, model, worktree, auto reply, failure handling).
