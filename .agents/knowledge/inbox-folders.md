# inbox-folders

How the Inbox's folders and groups treat conversations.

## r-Fi7Lg

A conversation filed in a folder leaves Scheduled, Earlier and Done (it is in its folder), but never leaves Needs you, Running or To review. Filing must never hide anything that needs the person or is working. Folders are paths (`Clients/Contigos` is a subfolder of `Clients`); deleting a folder deletes no conversation, they go back to the Inbox.

Evidence: the user asked for mailbox-style folders, subfolders, star and pin in the Inbox while insisting that nothing that needs review or attention is ever missed · Added 2026-11-18
