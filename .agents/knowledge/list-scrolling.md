# list-scrolling

How long lists of conversations, projects and workspaces are shown.

## r-Ls4nQ

Show conversations, projects and workspaces as one continuous list that scrolls, on the phone and on the desktop. Do not split them into pages with Previous/Next or a "1–8 of 80" pager, and do not size a list to the window so that rows hide behind a pager. Draw long lists in chunks as the person scrolls, and keep the scroll position when the list refreshes.

Evidence: the user, after the Paper redo (2.272.0–2.277.0) paged these lists: "I don't want pagination on the conversations/projects/workspaces, it should be a long scroll" · Added 2026-11-12
